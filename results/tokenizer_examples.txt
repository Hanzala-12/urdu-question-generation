Urdu morphology: the unigram model keeps common function words whole (کے، میں، سے) and breaks inflected / compound forms into a stem plus affix pieces (plural and oblique endings, the izafat 'ی'). Rare proper nouns fragment into near-character pieces, which is where most copy errors and <unk>-like behaviour come from.
